I am Pablo, a PhD student in Robotics at Carnegie Mellon University's Robotics Institute, advised by Dr. Jean Oh and Dr. Jonathan Francis. I am a member of the roBot Intelligence Group [(BIG)](https://cmubig.github.io/).

I like making things that work in the real world! My research develops frameworks for efficiently leveraging large pretrained models in embodied systems. Currently, I am distilling knowledge from conversational agentic frameworks and language into compact action representations for lightweight robot policy inference.

Along the way, I have been a research intern at the Bosch Center for Artificial Intelligence and Lavoro AI. Before my PhD, I received my bachelor's degree in Robotics and Digital Systems Engineering from Tec de Monterrey, Campus Guadalajara.
